## Part C 短篇阅读（读文章，答 3 题）

> Remote work was supposed to make our lives easier. In some ways, it did: no commuting, flexible hours, and more time with family. But a recent survey of 2,000 developers tells a different story. Nearly half of them say they now work longer hours than before, and one in three reports difficulty separating work from personal life. The reason, researchers suggest, is not the home office itself but the disappearing boundary between "on" and "off". When your laptop is always within reach, every evening looks like a possible work session. Companies that succeed at remote work, the survey finds, set clear rules: fixed start times, real lunch breaks, and a shared agreement that messages after 7 p.m. can wait until morning.

**C1** According to the survey, what problem do many developers report?

- A. They spend too much time with family.
- B. They work more hours and find it hard to switch off.
- C. They want to return to the office full-time.
- D. They dislike flexible hours.

**C2** What do researchers identify as the root cause?

- A. The home office is uncomfortable.
- B. Laptops are too slow for video meetings.
- C. The blurring line between work time and personal time.
- D. Companies force people to work at night.

**C3** Which practice does the survey associate with successful remote work?

- A. Answering messages immediately at any time.
- B. Clear boundaries such as fixed start times.
- C. Banning all work-related messages.
- D. Longer lunch breaks than office workers.

**Part A**（核验：词义锚定 + 同根词 / 语境替换双链）

| 题 | 答案 | 解析 |
|----|------|------|
| A1 | A | attend = be present at；attendance（出勤）同源验证 |
| A2 | A | acquire = gain by effort；acquisition（习得）同源验证；inquire / require 为形近干扰 |
| A3 | A | appeal to sb = attract sb；appealing（诱人的）同源验证 |
| A4 | A | adequate = enough for a purpose；形近干扰 adequate / adept，义近干扰 equal / accurate |
| A5 | A | vulnerable to attacks = 易受攻击；vulnerability（漏洞，CVE 里的 V）同源；venerable（可敬的）形近干扰 |

**Part B**（核验：规则依据 + 改写还原 / 排除法双链）

| 题 | 答案 | 解析 |
|----|------|------|
| G1 | B | for + 时间段且现在仍任职（I love my job）→ 现在完成；还原：joined three years ago and still here |
| G2 | B | 小偷消失发生在警察到达（过去）之前 → 过去的过去 → had done；时间轴验证 |
| G3 | B | 主将从现：if 条件从句用一般现在表将来；will 不得进入 if 条件从句（排除 A/D） |
| G4 | B | 还原：I spent time **in** the library → 地点状语 → where；which/that 需从句缺主宾，本句 spend time 不缺 |
| G5 | A | said 缺宾语 → what 既引导又作宾；还原：The thing (that) he said... |
| G6 | B | 非限制性定语从句指代整件事 → which；排除链：that 禁入非限制性，what 不引导定语从句，it 造成逗号粘连 |
| G7 | B | company 与 found（创立）为被动关系 → 过去分词 Founded；还原：The company **was founded** in 1998 |
| G8 | B | look forward to 中 to 是介词 → V-ing；对照：used to do（不定式）vs be used to doing |
| G9 | A | have sth **done** 使某事被做；report 与 send 为被动关系 → sent；干扰 D（have sb do 仅用于人） |
| G10 | A | bridge 与 build 被动 + in 2020 过去时间 → was built；and 并列 opened（过去式）互证 |
| G11 | A | worth doing 固定搭配，主动形式表被动；同构互证：need / require doing |
| G12 | B | right now 进行中 + bug 被 fix → 现在进行被动 is being fixed；排除 A（主动，bug 不能自己修）、C（主动 + has） |

**Part C**（核验：B 级原文定位，逐题标注定位句 + 干扰项错因）

| 题 | 答案 | 定位句 | 干扰项错因 |

| C1 | B | "work longer hours than before, and one in three reports difficulty separating work from personal life" | A 偷换对象（多陪家人是好处）；C 无中生有；D 偷换对象（flexible hours 是好处） |
