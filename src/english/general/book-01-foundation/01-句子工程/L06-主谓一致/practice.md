# L06 练习：主谓一致

> 配套 [第 6 讲](lesson.md)。基础层必做，强化层选做。答案在文末，做完再看。

---

## 基础层

**1. 选 is / are**：

1. The list of pending tasks ___ getting longer.
2. Every one of the tickets ___ assigned.
3. Ten minutes of debugging ___ enough for now.
4. Neither the backend nor the frontend APIs ___ ready.
5. Physics ___ not my favorite subject.
6. There ___ a cache server and three databases in the rack.
7. Nobody ___ answering the pager.
8. The team ___ divided on this design.（按成员个体）

**2. 找真主语**：划出主语，判断谓语该单还是复。

1. The number of open bugs is/are scary.
2. A number of bugs is/are still open.
3. The quality of the logs depends/depend on the verbosity.

**3. 改错**：

1. The list of tasks are long.
2. Neither of them are right.
3. Every developer and manager have a key.

**4. 课文理解与语法**：先读课文，再作答；每题只有一个正确选项。

1. 关于未修复 bug 的清单，课文怎么说？
   A. 已经很短了
   B. 比作者的手臂还长
   C. 只剩一个 bug
   D. 已经被全部修复
2. 负责人（our lead）在站会上说的话是什么意思？
   A. 每个人都负责这个问题的一部分
   B. 只有负责人负责全部
   C. 每个人都需要重启服务
   D. 每个人都不用喝咖啡
3. 为什么页面显示的是旧数据？
   A. 因为服务需要重启
   B. 因为数据库和缓存都没有刷新
   C. 因为数学算错了
   D. 因为计算器坏了
4. 「Neither the database nor the caches were refreshed.」谓语用 were，依据的原则是：
   A. 语法一致（主语单复数决定谓语）
   B. 意义一致（按意义而非形式）
   C. 就近原则（谓语跟最近的主语）
   D. 就远原则（谓语跟最远的主语）
5. 下列哪句主谓一致有误？
   A. Mathematics are hard.
   B. Every one of the services needs a restart.
   C. Nobody is drinking coffee.
   D. Ten minutes plus one forgotten test is a recipe for panic.
6. 不定代词 everybody / nobody / each 作主语时，谓语应采用：
   A. 复数形式
   B. 肯定式单数形式
   C. 与就近名词一致
   D. 用 be 动词

---

## 强化层

**5. 就近造句**：用 neither...nor 和 not only...but also 各造一句，让两个主语的单复数不同，体会就近原则。

**6. 短文判断**：下列 5 处画线部分，判断正误，错的改正。

> Everybody in our team have a clear role. The list of open bugs were long last week, but each of them is fixed now. There is two databases and a cache behind the firewall. Ten servers needs a restart, and neither the docs nor the runbook has been updated.

---

## 答案


**1.**
1. is — 真主语 list（of pending tasks 是修饰）
2. is — every one 单数
3. is — 时间段作整体（意义一致）
4. are — 就近原则：最近的主语 APIs 复数
5. is — 形复意单（学科名）
6. is — there be 就近：最近名词 a cache server 单数
7. is — nobody 单数
8. are — team 按成员个体理解（意义一致；作整体时用 is）

**2.**
1. The number（单）→ **is** — the number of + 复数名词 = 数量本身，单数
2. A number of bugs → **are** — a number of = 许多，真主语 bugs
3. The quality（单）→ **depends** — depends on 固定搭配，主语 quality

**3.**
1. The list of tasks **is** long. — 主语 list
2. Neither of them **is** right. — neither of + 复数正式用法取单数
3. Every developer and manager **has** a key. — every A and B 结构 = 单数

**4.**
1. B — 第 3 句：The list of open bugs is longer than my arm.
2. A — 第 6 句："Each of you owns a piece of this," our lead says at the standup.
3. B — 第 5 句：Neither the database nor the caches were refreshed, so the pages show old data.
4. C — 就近原则：neither...nor 谓语跟最近的主语 caches（复数）→ were。
5. A — 形复意单：Mathematics 作单数，应为 Mathematics is hard.
6. B — nobody / everybody / each 等不定代词作主语，谓语用肯定式单数（Nobody knows everything.）。

**5.**（主观题 — 参考要点）
- 要点覆盖示例：Neither the PM nor the developers were happy.（就近 developers）/ Not only the cache but also the database is corrupted.（就近 database 单数）
- 自检要点：谓语必须与**第二个**主语（最近者）一致；两句的两个主语单复数须不同，否则考查不到就近

**6.**
1. Everybody ... **has** a clear role — 不定代词单数
2. The list of open bugs **was** long — 主语 list，过去语境
3. ✓（each of them is fixed 正确）
4. There **are** two databases... — 就近 two databases 复数
5. Ten servers **need** a restart — 真主语 servers 复数（neither...nor... has been updated ✓ 就近 runbook 正确）
