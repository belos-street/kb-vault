## 1. 课文精读

**The Demo Is Tomorrow**

> How many things can go wrong before tomorrow's demo? Let me count. The list of open bugs is longer than my arm. Every one of the services needs a restart. Neither the database nor the caches were refreshed, so the pages show old data. "Each of you owns a piece of this," our lead says at the standup. Ten minutes plus one forgotten test is a recipe for panic, and there is less time than we think. Mathematics says we can make it; the calculator on my desk disagrees. Everybody is fixing something, and nobody is drinking coffee. Luckily, my butler never panics: it checks the caches every minute and sends me a summary.

**参考译文**

> 明天演示之前能出多少岔子？让我数数。未修复 bug 的清单比我的手臂还长。每一个服务都需要重启。数据库和缓存都没刷新过，所以页面显示旧数据。"你们每个人都拥有这个问题的一块，"负责人在站会上说。十分钟加一个被遗忘的测试，就是恐慌的配方，而时间比我们想的少。数学说我们能搞定；我桌上的计算器不同意。每个人都在修点什么，没人在喝咖啡。幸运的是，我的管家从不慌张：它每分钟检查一次缓存，还给我发摘要。

**关键句解析**

| 句子 | 拆解 |
|------|------|
| The **list** of open bugs **is** longer... | 真主语是 list（单数）— of open bugs 只是修饰，不许带偏谓语 |
| **Neither** the database **nor** the caches **were** refreshed... | neither...nor 就近原则：谓语跟最近的主语 caches（复数）→ were |
| **Every one** of the services **needs**... | every one 单数 → needs；"of the services" 是干扰 |
| **Ten minutes** plus one forgotten test **is**... | 时间 / 金钱作整体 = 单数（意义一致） |

## 3. 语法点拆解

### 3.1 三大原则

| 原则 | 内容 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| 语法一致 | 主语单复数决定谓语 | The list **is** long. |
| 意义一致 | 按意义而非形式 | Ten minutes **is** enough.（时间段 = 整体）；The team **is** winning.（整体） |
| 就近原则 | or / nor / either...or / not only...but also 跟**最近**的主语 | Neither he **nor they are** ready. |

### 3.2 高频陷阱表（先划掉修饰，再看主语）

| 陷阱 | ❌ 错误 | ✅ 正确 |
|------|--------|--------|
| 介词短语隔离 | The list of tasks **are** long. | The list of tasks **is** long. |
| every / each / no one | Every one of us **have** a key. | Every one of us **has** a key. |
| there be 就近 | There **are** a server and two clients. | There **is** a server and two clients.（be 跟最近的 a server） |
| 时间 / 金钱 | Ten dollars **are** enough. | Ten dollars **is** enough. |
| 形复意单 | Mathematics **are** hard. | Mathematics **is** hard.（news, physics 同理） |
| 不定代词 | Everybody **are** here. | Everybody **is** here.（nobody / each 同理） |

> there be 就近规则：be 的单复数跟**后面第一个名词**（There is a server and two clients ✅ / There are two clients and a server ✅）。

### 3.3 课文句标注

| 句子 | 触发原则 |
|------|---------|
| The list of bugs is longer... | 语法一致（主语 list） |
| Neither the database nor the caches were... | 就近原则（caches） |
| Each of you owns... | every / each + 单数 |
| Ten minutes plus one test is... | 意义一致（时间整体） |
| Mathematics says... / Everybody is... / Nobody is... | 形复意单 / 不定代词单数 |

## 4. 输出

1. **复述**：用 3 句复述课文，其中 1 句必须含 neither...nor 就近结构
2. **仿写（核心）**：描述你的项目现状 5 句，覆盖 5 个陷阱。参考起点：
   - The list of failed tests is getting shorter.
   - Every one of the services has its own database.
   - Neither the frontend nor the backend APIs are ready.
   - Two hours of debugging is enough for today.
   - Nobody likes a Friday deploy.
3. **自检**：每句先划掉介词短语，再检查谓语 — 你的真主语找对了吗？
