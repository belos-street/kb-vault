# L24：被动语态

> 学完本讲你能：**熟练地在主动与被动之间双向变形**，并判断哪些动词没有被动、什么时候该省 by 短语、什么时候主动形式就能表被动。

---

## 1. 为什么需要这个概念

主动句「谁做了什么」以施动者为中心；但很多时候我们关心的是**受事**（被做的事），或者根本不知道谁做的。被动语态把宾语提到主语位置：

| 说话焦点 | 选主动 / 被动 | 例 |
|---------|--------------|----|
| 强调受事 | 被动 | **The carrot** was eaten.（胡萝卜怎么样了？） |
| 施动者不明 / 不重要 | 被动 | The wolf **was caught** yesterday.（谁抓的不重要） |
| 客观语气（科技、新闻） | 被动 | The experiment **was done** in the forest. |

语态（voice）和时态（tense）是两套维度，可以叠加：`is eaten`（现在时 + 被动）、`has been eaten`（现在完成 + 被动）。

---

## 2. 核心概念与规则

### 2.1 构成：be + done（过去分词）

公式：`主语（受事）+ be（随时态变形）+ done + (by 施动者)`。

**只有及物动词（vt）才有被动** —— 因为被动 = 把宾语提前，没有宾语就没有可提前的对象。

### 2.2 各时态的被动形式

| 时态 | 被动形式 | 例 |
|------|---------|----|
| 一般现在 | am / is / are + done | The carrot **is eaten** every day. |
| 一般过去 | was / were + done | The carrot **was eaten** yesterday. |
| 一般将来 | will be + done | The carrot **will be eaten** tomorrow. |
| 过去将来 | would be + done | He said the carrot **would be eaten**. |
| 现在进行 | am / is / are being + done | The carrot **is being eaten** now. |
| 过去进行 | was / were being + done | The carrot **was being eaten** at 8. |
| 现在完成 | have / has been + done | The carrot **has been eaten**. |
| 过去完成 | had been + done | The carrot **had been eaten** before the wolf came. |
| 将来完成 | will have been + done | The carrot **will have been eaten** by noon. |

### 2.3 三类特殊被动变形

| 类型 | 主动 | 被动 | 要点 |
|------|------|------|------|
| 双宾语 | The wolf gave the rabbit a carrot. | The rabbit **was given** a carrot. / A carrot **was given** to the rabbit. | 两个宾语都能当主语；物作主语时间接宾语前补 to（give/show/send）或 for（buy/order/get） |
| 含复合宾语 | The carrot made the rabbit laugh. | The rabbit **was made to laugh** by the carrot. | 感官 / 使役动词（make/see/hear/watch）主动省 to，被动**还原 to** |
| 含介词的短语动词 | The wolf looked after the rabbit. | The rabbit **was looked after**. | 短语动词是整体，介词不能丢 |

### 2.4 没有被动的动词

| 类别 | 例 | 原因 |
|------|----|------|
| 不及物动词 | happen, appear, rise, arrive | 没有宾语可提前 |
| 表状态的 vi 短语 | belong to, consist of, last, break out | 同上 |
| 表「发生 / 出现」 | take place, occur | 同上 |

> `The accident was happened.` ❌ → `The accident happened.` ✅

### 2.5 主动形式表被动

| 句式 | 例 | 说明 |
|------|----|------|
| 主 + vt + 副词（well/easily） | The carrot **sells** well. | 主语是物，vi 形式表被动含义 |
| 感官系动词 + 形容词 | The carrot **tastes** good. | taste / smell / feel / sound / look |
| want / need / require + doing | The rabbit **needs washing**. | doing = to be done |
| be worth doing | The carrot **is worth eating**. | 同上 |

### 2.6 by 短语什么时候省

省略的两个条件：**施动者不知道**（The wolf was caught.）或**施动者不重要 / 不言自明**（The carrot was eaten.—— 显然是兔子吃的）。只有强调「谁做的」时才保留：The carrot was eaten **by the rabbit, not the wolf**.

---

## 3. 例句精讲

> The wolf gave the little rabbit two carrots.

按角色逐个变被动：

1. **物作主语**：`Two carrots were given to the little rabbit (by the wolf).`
2. **人作主语**：`The little rabbit was given two carrots (by the wolf).`
3. 若原句含使役：`The carrots made the rabbit happy.` → `The rabbit was made happy by the carrots.`；`made the rabbit eat` → `The rabbit was made **to** eat`（to 还原）。

叙事串起来：`The carrots were given to the rabbit. They were eaten happily. The rabbit was made fat by them.` —— 全程焦点在胡萝卜和兔子身上，狼退居幕后（by 短语全部省略）。

---

## 4. 常见坑

- ⚠️ **不及物动词变被动**：`The rabbit was happened.` ❌；`The accident was broken out.` ❌ —— 先问「这个动词有宾语吗」。
- ⚠️ **使役被动丢 to**：`The rabbit was made laugh.` ❌ → `was made to laugh` ✅（see/hear 同理：`was seen to enter`）。
- ⚠️ **短语动词拆掉介词**：`The rabbit was looked after` ✅，`was looked` ❌；`The plan was put off` ✅，`was put` ❌。
- ⚠️ **双宾被动漏 to**：`A carrot was given the rabbit.` ❌ → `A carrot was given **to** the rabbit.` ✅。
- ⚠️ **主动表被动误加 be**：`The carrot is tasted good.` ❌ → `The carrot tastes good.` ✅（taste 此处是系动词）。

---

## 5. 一句话总结

> 被动 = 受事主语 + be 随时态变形 + done：及物才有被动、使役还原 to、双宾两路变、vi 和系动词主动表被动 —— by 短语只在「施动者值得强调」时出场。

## 6. 随堂练习

**B1（选择）**：`The carrots will be delivered to the rabbit tomorrow.` 这句话的主动形式是？

- A. The rabbit will deliver the carrots tomorrow.
- B. The wolf will deliver the carrots to the rabbit tomorrow.
- C. The carrots will deliver the rabbit tomorrow.
- D. The rabbit will be delivered the carrots tomorrow.

**B2（填空）**：`The wolf considered the rabbit clever.` 变被动：`The rabbit was considered ______ (by the wolf).`

**S1（改错）**：The new carrot cake was tasted good by everyone. 找出错误并改正。

**S2（翻译）**：把下面句子改为以 the little rabbit 作主语的被动句：猎人昨天把这只小兔子送给了狼。

---

## 7. 参考答案与解析

- **B1**：**B**。物作主语的双宾被动 `…delivered to the rabbit`，还原主动时间接宾语 the rabbit 前要有 to，施动者是狼，故选 B。
- **B2**：**clever**。consider 含复合宾语（宾语 + 宾补），变被动时宾语 the rabbit 提为主语、宾补 clever 升为主补：was considered clever，补语原样保留、无需还原 to。
- **S1**：`was tasted good by everyone` → `tasted good to everyone`。taste 是感官系动词，主动形式表被动含义，没有被动语态，去掉 was 并把被动施事改用 to 引出。
- **S2**：**The little rabbit was given to the wolf by the hunter yesterday.** 主动形式是 give the little rabbit to the wolf，被动只需把受事 the little rabbit 提为主语，give 属 to 组，to the wolf 原样保留。
