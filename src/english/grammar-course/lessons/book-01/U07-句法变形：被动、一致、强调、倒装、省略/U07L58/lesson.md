# U07L58：省略与还原：四个公式

> 学完本课你能：**能读懂省略句并补全被省的成分**。

---

## 1. 本课目标

英语讨厌重复：上文刚说过的成分，下文再出现就省掉。省略让句子简洁，也让人「看不见主语、看不见谓语」——读长句卡壳，多半是**不知道省掉了什么**。总原则只有一条：**省掉的成分，语境里能明确补回来**；补不回来的不能省。今天背四个还原公式：①并列句还原（找连词，缺什么补什么）；②状语从句还原（when / if / though + 形容词 → 补主语 + be）；③不定式还原（句尾的 to 是钩子，必须保留）；④so / not 还原（`I think so` / `I hope not` 顶替整个从句）。

---

## 2. 课文

- The wolf tells his story in a few brief words.
  狼用几句简短的话讲了自己的故事。
- The little sheep asks if the shop will keep growing.
  小绵羊问小店会不会继续做下去。
- "I think so," answers the rabbit.{★ so 替代整个从句：I think so = I think that the shop will keep growing——上文说过的从句用 so 顶替，不重复}
  「我想会的，」兔子回答。
- The wolf says he hopes to stay in the forest, and everyone agrees.
  狼说他想留在森林里，大家都同意。
- Nobody worries about the old days any more.
  谁也不再为过去的日子担心。

- When young, the wolf knew the forest well.{★ 状语从句省略主语 + be：When young = When the wolf was young——从句主语与主句主语一致才可省}
  狼年轻时就很熟悉这片森林。
- The little sheep helps at the shop if necessary.{★ 固定省略：if necessary = if it is necessary——固定搭配整体省，见惯就认}
  必要时，小绵羊就来店里帮忙。
- The rabbit wants to write a new note, and the wolf wants to.{★ 不定式省略保留 to：wants to = wants to write a new note——to 是省略后看得见的钩子}
  兔子想写一张新字条，狼也想写。
- "Will the cold winter come soon?" the mouse asks.
  「冷冬天很快就来吗？」老鼠问。
- "I hope not," says the wolf.{★ not 替代否定从句：I hope not = I hope that the cold winter will not come soon——否定替代不能写 I don't hope so}
  「希望不会，」狼说。

- The old wolf is not afraid of the truth.
  老狼并不害怕真相。
- Nobody doubts the wolf now, and he expects no gifts.
  如今谁也不怀疑狼，他也不指望什么礼物。
- The animals nod to him one by one.
  动物们一个接一个地向他点头。
- The little sheep adds a word, and the wolf replies with a brief smile.
  小绵羊补了一句，狼用一个简短的微笑作答。
- Everyone supposes the shop will be safe from now on.
  大家都觉得，小店从此就安稳了。

## 4. 语法点精讲

**规则**：省略 = 省掉语境能补回的重复件。**四个还原公式**：①**并列句还原**：找并列连词（and / but / or），看两侧缺什么补什么——左边有主谓、右边只有主语和宾语，就补回重复的谓语（`The wolf ate a carrot and the little sheep an apple.` 补回 ate）；②**状语从句还原**：从句只剩「when / if / though + 形容词 / 分词」时补 `主语 + be`，且**从句主语必须与主句一致**（`When young, the wolf knew the forest well.` = When the wolf was young …）；固定省略照单全收（`if necessary` = if it is necessary）；③**不定式还原**：句尾孤零零一个 `to`，补回 `to + 动词`——**to 必须保留**，它是省略后看得见的钩子（`The wolf wants to.` = wants to write a new note）；④**so / not 还原**：`I think so` = I think that …，`I hope not` = I hope that … not——so 顶肯定从句、not 顶否定从句，hope / be afraid 两边都能接（`I hope so` / `I hope not`），否定不写 I don't hope so。

例句（✅ 正确 / ❌ 错误对照）：

- ✅ `When young, the wolf knew the forest well.` —— 还原 When the wolf was young，从句主语与主句一致才能省
- ✅ `The rabbit wants to write a note, and the wolf wants to.` —— 保留 to，补回 to write a note
- ❌ `When hungry, the carrot looked delicious.` —— carrot 不会 hungry，主语不一致不能省，要补出 When the wolf was hungry

**B1（选择）**：「Will the wolf leave? — I hope not.」里的 not 顶替什么？
- A. 一个单词
- B. 整个否定从句：I hope that the wolf will not leave
- C. 主语 the wolf
- D. 谓语 leave

**B2（选择）**：「When young, the wolf knew the forest well.」还原后是哪句？
- A. When the wolf was young, the wolf knew the forest well.
- B. When young was the wolf, the wolf knew the forest well.
- C. When the wolf young, the wolf knew the forest well.
- D. When the forest was young, the wolf knew it well.

### 5.2 操练（替换）

**P1（操练）**：When young, the wolf knew the forest well. = When ______ young, the wolf knew the forest well.（提示：主语 + be）

**P2（操练）**：The rabbit can eat the carrot if he wants to. = if he wants to ______ the carrot.（提示：补回动词 eat）

**P3（操练）**：The wolf ate a carrot and the little sheep an apple. = and the little sheep ______ an apple.（提示：重复的谓语）

## 8. 参考答案与解析

- **B1**：**B**。not 顶替整个否定从句：I hope not = I hope that the wolf will not leave；so 顶替肯定从句，不能用 I don't hope so。
- **B2**：**A**。状语从句省略「主语 + be」：When young = When the wolf was young，且从句主语与主句主语 the wolf 一致。
- **P1**：**the wolf was**。状语从句还原公式：when + 形容词 = when + 主语 + be；主语取主句主语 the wolf。
- **P2**：**eat**。不定式省略只省动词及其宾语，保留 to 这个钩子：if he wants to = if he wants to eat the carrot。
- **P3**：**ate**。并列句还原：and 后省掉了与前句相同的谓语 ate，补回即 and the little sheep ate an apple。
- **S1**：参考：**Will the shop keep growing? I think so. / Will the wolf leave? I hope not. / When young, the wolf knew the forest well.（还原：When the wolf was young, the wolf knew the forest well.）**（省略与还原对上即可）。
- **Q1**：**B**。not 只顶替从句：I hope not = I hope that the wolf will not leave；否定替代不写 I don't hope so，也不写 I hope that not。
- **Q2**：**be**。状语从句省略省掉「主语 + be」（When young = When the wolf was young），主语不一致就不能省。
- **Q3**：**B**。不定式省略必须保留 to：if you want to = if you want to eat the carrot。
- **Q4**：**not**。be afraid 的否定替代用 not：I'm afraid not = I'm afraid that it will not happen；肯定才用 so。
- **Q5**：**B**。never 开头、助动词 have 提到主语前，只提前助动词 = 部分倒装（U07L57）。
- **Q6**：**is**。the number of …（……的数量）作主语，谓语用单数 is growing（U07L55）。
